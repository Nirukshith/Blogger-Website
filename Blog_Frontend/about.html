About Truman Writes

Truman Writes is a personal blog created to share writing, ideas and tutorials about web development and design. The blog focuses on clear, practical posts that help beginners and intermediate developers become more productive.

Who writes here?

This site is maintained by a solo author who enjoys building small web projects, learning new technologies, and writing about the process. Posts include tutorials, opinion pieces, and project walkthroughs.
